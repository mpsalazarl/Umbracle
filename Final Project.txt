Goal:
create an umbracle for a botanic inner space, for different range of interior plants.
Using fractal geometry for the structure and the shading elements.
the shading elements should be kinetic, the motion of these elements allow to control the inner light, temperature.
the space is divided by areas to accomodate different plant species: tropical, mediterranean and desertic species

tropical plants:
temp: 15º night/25º day
hum: 70%
light hours: 10 to 12

desertic plants:
temp: 12º at night/ 30º day
hum: 30%
light hours: 6 to 8

mediterranean:
temp: temp: 15º night/25º day
hum: 50%
light hours: 6 t0 10

Pseudocode:

1- start creating 3 circles of 70sqm, disposed on an equilateral triangle, the circles could be intersected, this intersection could be controled defining the side of the triangle
2- populate this circles, each one of them with a number of points that can  be controlled by sliders imputed on A, B, and C.
3- draw a circle from the center of each point, from each circular region, with radious that goes randomly from 2 to 5 meters.
4- draw the boundary of all of this circles inside of each circular region, make the curve/curve intersection of this circles, and keep the segments of the ones that are on the boundary.
5- create a continous curve from this circular sections, smoothing the joins with a fillet
